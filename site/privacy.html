Privacy Policy

The short version: your quiz answers never leave your device. We don't have accounts, we don't ask for your name or email, and we don't send your answers to any server.

  • Quiz answers: stored only in your browser's temporary session storage so we can show your result. They disappear when you close the tab.
  • Language preference: stored locally in your browser so the site remembers your choice.
  • Analytics & ads: if we add analytics or advertising in the future, this policy will be updated and the providers will be listed here.

Questions about privacy? See the contact page.
